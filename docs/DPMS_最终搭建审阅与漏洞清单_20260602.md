## 2026-06-03 Account Safety Governance Update

This section is intentionally written in ASCII to avoid terminal encoding drift in the recovered project.

### New account safety capability

- `GET /api/accounts/` now includes `latest_risk_event` for each account.
- `POST /api/accounts/health/recheck` adds a conservative account recovery pass:
  - `ready` accounts without credentials are moved to `login_required`.
  - stale `executing` accounts are moved to `cooling` and logged as `stale_execution`.
  - `cooling` accounts are restored to `ready` only when credentials exist, cooldown elapsed, proxy cooldown elapsed, and no recent risk event exists.
  - `login_required`, `frozen`, and `banned` accounts are not auto-restored.
- Account state transitions now validate the current state and reject invalid flows such as `login_required -> ready`.
